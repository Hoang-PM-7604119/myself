## Overview

The Bee Monitoring AIoT System supports hive monitoring, bee counting, and environmental sensing through a mix of embedded devices, backend services, and computer-vision modules.

## Role

Minh-Hoang coordinated a multidisciplinary student team responsible for embedded development, backend services, computer vision, and integration. The project was funded by Vietnam's Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Development.

## Outcome

The project turned research requirements into a working AIoT platform and shaped Minh-Hoang's later focus on deployable environmental sensing systems.

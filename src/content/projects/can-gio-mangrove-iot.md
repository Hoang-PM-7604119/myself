## Overview

The Can Gio project develops environmental monitoring infrastructure for long-term mangrove conservation. The work centers on reliable sensing, remote access, deployment planning, and maintainable field operations.

## Role

Minh-Hoang leads student developers and research assistants through system design, procurement planning, deployment activities, and integration of remote sensing architecture.

## Engineering Focus

The technical challenge is to keep sensing, networking, and data workflows dependable in a field setting where access, weather, and maintenance windows are constrained.
